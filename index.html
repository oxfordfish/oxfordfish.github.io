⚡ STM32 Reverse Osmosis Controller (ROC) Board

In Fabrication / Untested

A custom industrial 2-layer PCB designed around the STM32G0F6Px microcontroller, featuring an integrated 24V-to-3.3V step-down buck converter, low-side MOSFET drivers, and hardware-conditioned sensor inputs for automated RO system management.

  • Actuator Control (Low-Side Drivers): Dedicated MOSFET outputs with flyback protection for high-pressure booster pump, inlet solenoid, and flush solenoid switching.
  • Power & Conversion: On-board 24V to 3.3V high-efficiency buck converter switching regulator supplying system logic and sensors.
  • Conditioned Sensor Inputs: RC-filtered voltage divider stages for high/low pressure switches and integrated TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) probe monitoring.
  • Debugging & Hardware Design: Accessible 4-pin SWD header for flashing/debugging, bottom-side silkscreen polarity labeling, engineered using KiCad 10.0.
